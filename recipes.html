Methods

30 easy air fryer recipes

Short, reliable methods with the same temperatures as the chart. Add sauce after cooking when you want the crust to stay crisp.

01

Crispy Chicken Wings

400°F • 20–26 min

  1. Pat dry, season, and air fry, shaking twice.
  2. Sauce after cooking so the skin stays crisp.

02

Perfect Salmon

390°F • 8–12 min

  1. Place skin-side down. Do not flip.
  2. Cook to 145°F.

03

Frozen Fries

400°F • 12–18 min

  1. Spread in a single layer.
  2. Shake every 5 minutes.

04

Garlic Butter Steak Bites

400°F • 6–10 min

  1. Cube and season the steak.
  2. Air fry, then toss in garlic butter.

05

Roasted Broccoli

375°F • 8–12 min

  1. Toss with oil and garlic.
  2. Shake halfway.

06

Honey Garlic Shrimp

400°F • 5–8 min

  1. Cook the shrimp first.
  2. Toss in honey-garlic sauce after.

07

Crispy Chickpeas

400°F • 12–18 min

  1. Dry well and season.
  2. Shake until crunchy.

08

Pork Chops

400°F • 12–16 min

  1. Season and flip halfway.
  2. Pull at 145°F and rest.

09

Chicken Breast

375°F • 15–20 min

  1. Season and flip halfway.
  2. Cook to 165°F.

10

Bacon

350–400°F • 7–10 min

  1. Lay in a single layer.
  2. No flip needed.

11

Brussels Sprouts

375°F • 12–18 min

  1. Halve, oil, and shake until the edges caramelize.

12

Sweet Potato Fries

380°F • 12–18 min

  1. Cut, oil lightly, and shake twice.

13

Frozen Nuggets

400°F • 10–12 min

  1. Cook from frozen and shake halfway.

14

Mozzarella Sticks

380°F • 6–8 min

  1. Watch closely so the cheese does not leak.

15

Burgers

375°F • 10–14 min

  1. Flip halfway. Do not press the patty.

16

Steak (1-inch)

400°F • 8–14 min

  1. Flip halfway and rest 5 minutes.

17

Cod or White Fish

380°F • 8–12 min

  1. Light oil. Check a minute or two early.

18

Cauliflower

375°F • 10–15 min

  1. Toss with spices and shake halfway.

19

Asparagus

400°F • 6–9 min

  1. Toss with oil. It finishes fast.

20

Baked Potato

400°F • 35–45 min

  1. Pierce the skin before it goes in.

21

Tater Tots

400°F • 12–15 min

  1. Shake halfway.

22

Onion Rings

380°F • 8–10 min

  1. Shake halfway.

23

Chicken Drumsticks

380°F • 20–25 min

  1. Flip halfway.

24

Sausage Links

380°F • 10–14 min

  1. Shake halfway.

25

Meatballs

380°F • 10–12 min

  1. Shake halfway so every side browns.

26

Green Beans

375°F • 8–12 min

  1. Shake halfway.

27

Zucchini

400°F • 8–12 min

  1. Slice evenly and use a light oil.

28

Hard-Boiled Eggs

270°F • 15–17 min

  1. Move straight to an ice bath.

29

Garlic Bread

360°F • 4–6 min

  1. Watch it. Butter browns quickly.

30

Reheating Pizza

350°F • 3–5 min

  1. A few minutes crisps the bottom without drying the cheese.
