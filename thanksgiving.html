Thanksgiving air fryer recipes

Faster sides and a turkey breast that fits the basket. Times assume a preheated air fryer.

Air Fryer Turkey Breast

350°F • 40–55 minutes • Serves 4–6

  1. Pat a 2–3 lb turkey breast dry and season with salt, pepper, sage, and garlic.
  2. Brush lightly with oil or melted butter.
  3. Air fry at 350°F, flipping halfway, until the center reaches 165°F.
  4. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.

Crispy Brussels Sprouts

375°F • 12–18 minutes

  1. Halve the sprouts and toss with oil, salt, and a little maple syrup or balsamic.
  2. Air fry at 375°F, shaking halfway, until the edges are caramelized.

Sweet Potato Cubes

380–400°F • 15–20 minutes

  1. Peel, cube, and toss with oil, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt.
  2. Air fry at 380–400°F, shaking twice, until tender.

Stuffing Balls

350°F • 12–15 minutes

  1. Roll leftover or prepared stuffing into golf-ball portions.
  2. Spray lightly with oil and air fry at 350°F until golden and hot.

Green Bean Almondine

375°F • 8–12 minutes

  1. Toss green beans with oil, salt, and garlic powder.
  2. Air fry at 375°F, shaking halfway.
  3. Finish with toasted sliced almonds and lemon.

Cranberry Turkey Meatballs

380°F • 10–14 minutes

  1. Mix ground turkey with breadcrumbs, egg, salt, pepper, and a spoonful of cranberry sauce.
  2. Air fry at 380°F until the center reaches 165°F.

Honey Roasted Carrots

380°F • 12–15 minutes

  1. Toss carrot sticks with oil, salt, and a drizzle of honey.
  2. Air fry at 380°F, shaking halfway, until glazed.

Mashed Potato Cakes

375°F • 10–12 minutes

  1. Shape leftover mashed potatoes into patties and dust lightly with flour.
  2. Spray with oil and air fry at 375°F until both sides are golden.
