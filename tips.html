Before you cook

Air fryer tips and FAQ

The chart times work when the basket is hot, the food is dry, and you stop at temperature — not when the timer yells.

Always preheat

Run it empty for 2–3 minutes. A cold start cooks the outside before the middle.

Don’t crowd the basket

A single layer crisps. Piling food steams it. Cook in batches.

Pat food dry

Moisture fights crisp. Dry meats and vegetables before oil and salt.

Common questions

How long do you air fry chicken breast?

A boneless chicken breast takes 15–20 minutes at 375°F. Flip halfway and pull it at 165°F. Thicker pieces need the long end of that range.

What temperature for frozen french fries?

400°F for 12–18 minutes. Shake the basket every 5 minutes so the fries crisp instead of steaming.

Do I need to preheat an air fryer?

Yes, for the times on this chart. Run it empty for 2–3 minutes. A cold start cooks unevenly and the listed times will run long.

Can I cook frozen food from frozen?

Yes. Fries, nuggets, tots, and most breaded frozen foods go in straight from the freezer. Do not thaw them first.

Why is my air fryer food soggy?

The basket is usually too full, or the food went in wet. Pat it dry, use a single layer, and save thick sauces for after cooking.

What internal temperatures should I use?

Chicken and turkey 165°F. Pork chops and steaks 145°F, then rest. Fish 145°F. A thermometer beats the timer.

How do I clean an air fryer?

Let it cool. Soak the basket in warm soapy water. Wipe the chamber with a damp cloth. Skip steel wool on nonstick. Baking soda paste lifts stuck-on bits.
